Method

Controlled comparison

  • Identical prompt set, system policy, temperature, max tokens and seed handling for both variants.
  • Raters score without knowing which variant produced the output.
  • Refusals are classified by rubric, not by keyword matching alone.
  • Disallowed-category samples are reported as aggregates with an evaluator rationale — never as raw text.
  • Each run is versioned with the checkpoint hash, quantisation and dataset id.
Reading the result

Why refusal rate is not the headline

Abliteration suppresses the learned direction associated with refusals. It is not a general quality improvement, and it can alter reliability and formatting discipline alongside safety behaviour.

One published abliterated model card reported refusals falling from 98% to 39% on its own 100-prompt harmful test set. A lower refusal count says nothing about whether the remaining answers are accurate, well-formatted, or safe — which is why this dashboard always pairs refusal rate with helpful completion, unsafe completion and format compliance.

Product rule. A refusal-reduced profile is an explicitly labelled research configuration with its own workspace and audit tag. It is never the default for a business workspace, and it is never sold as the commercial proposition.
