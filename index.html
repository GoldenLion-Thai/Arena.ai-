How it works

Four steps, all inside your boundary.

Connect approved documents

Point at the sources you have cleared for use. Nothing else is reachable by retrieval.

Index inside your environment

Chunk, hash and embed locally or in your VPC. Chunks carry offsets so citations resolve to an exact span.

Chat with a chosen model

Pick per conversation. The picker shows location, speed, context, memory and policy profile before you send.

Keep references and controls attached

Sources, token counts, latency and access decisions travel with the transcript for audit.

Honesty note. Every privacy claim on this page is a design target for the reference architecture, not a warranty. Before publishing: confirm your gateway disables provider training in writing, verify data residency for inference and embeddings, test that deletion removes vector-index chunks (not just records), and have counsel review the retention and DSAR position. The default responder in the demo workspace is an on-device stub — point Settings → Model gateway at your own Ollama or vLLM endpoint for real inference. “Abliterated” configurations are labelled research profiles and are never a default.
